Livestock Vitality

Cattle Healthcare & Livestock Wellness

Proper livestock healthcare is vital for dairy farm productivity in Bihar. Cows and buffaloes are vulnerable to nutritional gaps, calcium depletion post-calving, and digestive stress caused by feed variations. Addressing these issues with appropriate mineral mixtures, liver tonics, and lactation promoters prevents drop-offs in yield and improves herd lifecycle health.

VIRBAC VETCARE PVT LTD supplies general wellness lines that assist Patna dairy farmers in meeting animal nutrition benchmarks naturally and effectively.

Targeted Support

Cow & Buffalo Healthcare Management

Different breeds and species require customized wellness tracking to maintain health and farm efficiency.

Cow Care & Nutrition

Focuses on Sahiwal, Gir, and crossbred dairy cattle. Supplies include calcium enrichment, mineral supplements, and rumen bypass fat products to optimize lactation peaks, maintain body condition score (BCS), and support postpartum recovery.

🐃

Buffalo Care & Digestibility

Aimed at Murrah and local buffalo breeds. Buffaloes have unique fat synthesis pathways and require higher dietary fat and fiber digestibility. Our distributed feed formulations aid in fat composition yield and protect against ruminal acidosis.

Medical Safety

Veterinary Support & Responsible Animal Care

Responsible livestock farming requires absolute adherence to veterinary guidelines. Misuse of veterinary formulations can lead to antibiotic resistance, milk residue risks, and compromised animal health.

We supply healthcare items strictly through licensed veterinary professionals and chemist retail counters. We advocate that cattle owners always consult qualified veterinary doctors in Patna for medical diagnosis and correct dosage instructions.
